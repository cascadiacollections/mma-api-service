About MMA Pick'em

What it does

MMA Pick'em is a lightweight, independent tool for choosing fight winners, sharing a compact link with friends, and grading those selections when official results become available.

How sharing works

Picks are encoded into the URL fragment in the browser. The fragment is not sent with the page request, and the service does not require an account or store a server-side pick history.

What it is not

The project is not a sportsbook, gambling service, contest operator, or official source of fight information. It accepts no wagers or entry fees and awards no prizes.

Open source

The application is open source under the MIT License. Technical details, issue tracking, and contribution guidance are available in the GitHub repository.
