# Legal launch checklist

This checklist is operational guidance, not legal advice. Complete it before
commercial promotion, advertising, sponsorship, paid access, prizes, or
material traffic growth.

## Data rights

- [ ] Review the then-current Disney/ESPN terms with qualified counsel.
- [ ] Obtain written permission or replace the undocumented feed with a licensed
      sports-data provider before commercial launch.
- [ ] Confirm the licensed fields, caching periods, display rights, territories,
      attribution, and termination requirements.
- [ ] Keep the provider adapter isolated and retain an immediate disable/remove
      path.
- [ ] Do not circumvent authentication, CAPTCHA, rate limits, paywalls, IP
      blocks, or other technical controls.

## Branding and content

- [ ] Keep the product name and trade dress distinct from UFC and ESPN.
- [ ] Use UFC only to identify the events covered; never use “official,” partner,
      sponsor, or endorsement language without authorization.
- [ ] Obtain permission or an appropriate license before relying on third-party
      fighter photography in a commercial deployment. Direct CDN loading,
      attribution, and a source link do not themselves grant reuse rights.
- [ ] Do not add UFC/ESPN logos, broadcast footage, promotional copy, fighter
      likenesses, or proprietary statistics without licenses.
- [ ] Keep the non-affiliation disclosure visible in the product.

## Contest structure

- [ ] Keep entry free and award nothing of value unless promotions/gaming counsel
      approves official rules, eligibility, geography, age, registration, and
      tax requirements.
- [ ] Do not facilitate wagers or market grades as betting advice.
- [ ] Re-review state and national law before adding leaderboards with prizes,
      subscriptions tied to contests, or sponsor-funded awards.

## Privacy and operations

- [ ] Publish deployment-specific contact information and log-retention periods.
- [ ] Confirm production infrastructure does not log URL fragments.
- [ ] Add a data-processing inventory before accounts, analytics, email,
      geolocation, or payment information are introduced.
- [ ] Establish a private channel for legal and rights-holder notices.
- [ ] Re-review Terms and Privacy notices whenever product behavior changes.

## Launch approval

- [ ] Obtain written legal approval for the intended jurisdictions and business
      model.
- [ ] Record the data license, trademark review, privacy review, and contest-law
      decision in a private launch record.
